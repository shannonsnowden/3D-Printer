THANKSGIVING LANTERN PANELS — FOR THE USER-SPECIFIED 78 mm SLOT SPAN

Six panels: turkey, cornucopia, pumpkin, falling leaves, pilgrim hat, pumpkin pie.
Each panel is 77.5 mm wide x 105 mm high x 1.8 mm maximum thickness.
77.5 mm allows 0.5 mm total nominal width clearance within the specified 78 mm span.

Only X (width) was scaled: 84.4 -> 77.5 mm, factor 0.9182464455.
Original artwork is retained, compressed horizontally. Height and thickness were
not scaled: continuous glow sheet remains 0.7 mm, detail floors 1.1 mm, maximum
relief/rails 1.8 mm. No global slicer scaling. Original base, roof and panels
were not overwritten. Tiny inherited mesh-edge artifacts on three designs were
normalized to produce watertight outputs without moving the remaining vertices.
